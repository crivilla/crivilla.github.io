I am a Health Engineer passionate about leveraging data to drive insights in the healthcare sector, contributing to enhanced patient care and outcomes.

I have been immersed in the world of software and clinical data since I started my Health Engineering degree in 2016. Within my degree, I studied a specialty in Clinical Informatics, focusing on the fields I am most interested in: software development, data mining and health information systems. 

Since then, I have navigated through diverse projects and technologies, working both in the public sector, in research and also on recommendations for policymaking; and in the private sector as part of a leading Medtech company focused on services processes and BI in an international environment.

I embrace challenges, finding joy in tackling problems with a positive and constructive mindset, and focus on assisting my colleagues effectively, drawing from the positive leadership experiences I am so grateful to have encountered so far.
